About Royal Meats

A family name you can taste

We are butchers, bakers and grill people. This is how Royal Meats came to be.

Our family story

Where the crown comes from

Every family has a dish that brings everyone together. For ours, it was ćevapi on the grill: the smell of smoke on a summer evening, warm somun, and far too many people around one table.

We named the business Royal Meats because that is how we wanted every customer to feel: treated like royalty, and fed like family. The crown on our R is a promise. The fork is a reminder that it all ends up on the grill.

  1. The beginning

    A family ćevapi recipe, a backyard grill and a line of neighbors asking for more.

  2. The counter

    We opened our first storefront with a butcher case, a bakery oven and a promise to do it the old way.

  3. The smokehouse

    Demand for our sausages and smoked beef led to our own production kitchen and beechwood smokehouse.

  4. Today

    Royal Meats packs are now carried by partner grocers and restaurants across the region.

Our kitchen & smokehouse

Made in-house, in small batches

Our production kitchen is where ćevapi are formed, sausages are stuffed and smoked, and our deli meats are cured and sliced. Small batches mean we taste everything, and nothing leaves until it is right.

  • Natural beechwood smoke
  • Family spice blends

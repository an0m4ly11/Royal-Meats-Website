Stuffed Burger

A Balkan-style burger with a molten kajmak center. Your backyard will never be the same.

Made with Royal Meats Burger Patties

Ingredients

  • 4 Royal Meats Burger Patties
  • 4 tbsp kajmak or cream cheese
  • 4 lepinja breads
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • Ajvar and tomato, to serve

Method

  1. 1

    Press a spoon of kajmak into the middle of each patty and fold the edges over to seal.

  2. 2

    Grill over medium heat 5 to 6 minutes per side.

  3. 3

    Toast the lepinja, then layer ajvar, the patty, onion and tomato.
